# Izge

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Company decision-makers evaluating Izge, and employees coordinating daily work using company evidence. Administrators govern workspace access and sources.

## Product Purpose
Turn scattered company records into evidence-backed understanding, discoveries, and next actions. Conversations are one interface, not the entire product.

## Operating Context
Employees collaborate in team chat, involve @izge, ask privately, inspect source evidence, and review findings. The website should demonstrate a consequential discovery alongside everyday collaboration.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing source code implements team conversations, private assistant conversations, source previews, memory relationships and review findings. Evidence access remains permission-scoped. Do not imply discoveries come from nothing: show the otherwise-overlooked connection between existing records.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve Izge identity and existing logo. Present the product through convincing interactions rather than long explanatory copy. Premium and memorable without invented claims.

## Evidence on Hand
The repository's Pusula/Atlas demonstration fixture contains contract, invoice, and delivery records supporting 18 hours at 2,000 TL: 36,000 TL plus VAT in potential additional billing, subject to commercial approval. This is a demo scenario, not a customer result or a confirmed receivable. Paths: backend/manual_eval/pusula_demo/preloaded/01_atlas_hizmet_sozlesmesi.md, 02_atlas_eylul_faturasi.md, 03_atlas_ek_is_teslim_ve_gunlukler.md.

## Product Principles
- Show the evidence behind a surprising discovery.
- Connect discovery to a human decision, not autonomous promises.
- Demonstrate collaboration and proactive value, not chat alone.
- Keep company/employee flows separate from administrator operations.

## Delivery Constraints
Website redesign only. Image-first concepts require approval before implementation. No deployment, Railway changes, live-site edits, commit, or push without explicit authorization. Preserve unrelated working-tree changes. Keep English and Turkish support.

## Website direction confirmed
The user chose the dark product-led direction on the local decision page (discovery). Product truth outranks generated embellishments: reuse actual app components or their presentation patterns, and permit small shared-component polish only when grounded in existing behavior. No idealized capabilities, integration labels, invented finding counts, or fictitious source formats. The original composition remains an available choice in the final comp round.
