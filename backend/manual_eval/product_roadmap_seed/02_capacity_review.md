# Capacity review

Date: 2026-02-20

The product and operations leads reviewed actual onboarding load after the January roadmap reset.

## Outcomes
Manual onboarding volume reached 68 accounts per month and response times slipped to nine days.
Support escalations increased after onboarding delays.

## Constraints
Growth experiment work cannot be paused, so engineering capacity remains capped through the end of Q2.

## Assumptions
The team still believed manual onboarding could bridge the gap until Q3.
