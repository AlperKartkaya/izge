# Onboarding strategy reset

Date: 2026-01-10

The product team reviewed onboarding performance and roadmap sequencing for the first half of the year.

## Decision
We decided to move the self-serve onboarding launch target to Q3 2026 and prioritize foundation work first.

## Assumptions
We assumed manual onboarding could absorb demand through Q2 if monthly volume stayed under 40 accounts.

## Constraints
The team was limited to five engineers and one designer through Q2.

## Commitments
Ravi will publish a revised onboarding roadmap by 2026-01-14.
