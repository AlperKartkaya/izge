# Northstar Payroll enterprise launch baseline

Date: 2026-01-15

Northstar Payroll is a B2B payroll-compliance platform preparing an enterprise onboarding launch for regulated employers.

## Decision
We decided to launch Northstar Payroll enterprise onboarding in Q3 2026 after payroll ledger reconciliation and audit-log instrumentation are complete.

## Assumptions
We assumed support could manually onboard up to 35 enterprise employers per month until the Q3 2026 launch.

## Constraints
Northstar Payroll must retain payroll change audit logs for seven years and cannot expose employee tax identifiers in generated evidence packets.
The enterprise onboarding launch requires payroll ledger reconciliation before broad release.

## Commitments
Product lead Maya will publish the Northstar Payroll enterprise onboarding roadmap by 2026-02-05.
