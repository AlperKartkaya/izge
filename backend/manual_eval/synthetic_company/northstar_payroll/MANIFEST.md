# Northstar Payroll synthetic company corpus

This one-company corpus models a realistic B2B payroll-compliance launch with:

- a Q3 launch baseline;
- compliance/security constraints;
- support-capacity assumption drift;
- a late-Q2 leadership reversal;
- a LedgerBridge migration RFC;
- customer escalation and launch checkpoint evidence;
- one prompt-injection artifact; and
- one restricted confidential risk memo.

The eval uploads the first eight artifacts as workspace-visible evidence and creates the confidential memo through the restricted pasted-text path.
