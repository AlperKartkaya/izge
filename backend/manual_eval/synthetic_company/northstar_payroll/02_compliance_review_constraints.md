# Northstar Payroll compliance review constraints

Date: 2026-02-20

The compliance and security review tested whether the enterprise onboarding launch could pass SOC 2 and payroll privacy checks.

## Outcomes
The compliance review increased security approval confidence because audit-log instrumentation captured payroll administrator actions.
The review also revealed that payroll ledger reconciliation lacked dual-control approval for off-cycle pay runs.

## Constraints
The enterprise onboarding launch must pass SOC 2 control evidence review before broad release.
Security review must verify that generated answers cite source artifacts and do not expose raw storage paths or employee tax identifiers.

## Assumptions
We expected dual-control approval to be added by the platform team before 2026-03-20.
