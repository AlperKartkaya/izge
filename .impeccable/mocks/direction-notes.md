# Website direction exploration

Audience scene: a company leader deciding whether employees can stop losing consequential context between daily conversations and records.
Mechanism: connect accessible company evidence to surface an overlooked discrepancy and bring it into the working conversation.
Rut: centered SaaS slogan above a chat screenshot; predictable opposite: abstract cinematic memory art with no demonstrable outcome.

Seven grounded candidates, ordered by resonance:
1. The moment of discovery: a dark screening-room reveal of one live product finding; pearl type and quiet blue evidence cues. A product-native stage gives the discovery room while surrounding chrome stays quiet.
2. Reconciliation sheet: crisp cool-white accounting comparison and dark ink, with discrepancy annotations. Contract, work log and invoice converge into a reviewable finding.
3. Incident chronology: broadcast-quality replay timeline, navy and warm-white type, a single amber event marker. Repeated support incidents reveal the unfinished root cause.
4. The handover: translucent dividers from contemporary workplace architecture, slate and frosted white with clear humanist sans. A conversation hands context into a source and a next action.
5. Executive briefing: blue-white presentation-room clarity, large sober sans and compact evidence captions. A discovery is demonstrated before an employee work session unfolds.
6. Proof overlay: registration layers from technical publishing, white and indigo with precise grotesk type. Isolated records align and expose the missing invoice line.
7. Margin intelligence: annotated professional reading, pale mineral paper and graphite with restrained blue notes. An overlooked promise emerges from the source margin into team work.

No customer testimonial, revenue guarantee, independent AI action, or unsupported provider claim. The displayed finding must say demo scenario and potential amount pending approval.
