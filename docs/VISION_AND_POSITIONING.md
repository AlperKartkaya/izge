# VISION_AND_POSITIONING.md
## Institution Memory Engine

## Product definition
A local-first organizational reasoning system that reconstructs why decisions were made, what changed over time, what assumptions are stale, where contradictions exist, and what the organization should do next.

This is not a chatbot.
This is not document search.
This is not generic AI note-taking.

This is a system for turning fragmented organizational history into decision intelligence.

## The real problem
Most organizations do not fail because information is missing.
They fail because memory is broken.

Symptoms:
- the same debates happen repeatedly,
- teams contradict old decisions without realizing it,
- postmortem lessons do not propagate,
- roadmap commitments conflict with current plans,
- historical context is trapped in scattered documents and conversations,
- assumptions made months ago continue to shape decisions long after they stopped being valid.

## Product thesis
Organizations need a system that can answer:
- what did we decide,
- who decided it,
- why did we decide it,
- what constraints shaped the decision,
- what happened afterward,
- what assumptions no longer hold,
- what current proposals conflict with historical reality,
- what we should do now given what history shows.

## Product philosophy
- Memory before magic
- History before speculation
- Structure from chaos
- Decision intelligence over retrieval
- Local-first trust

## Positioning sentence
A local-first AI system that reconstructs organizational decision history, detects contradictions and stale assumptions, and recommends better next actions.
