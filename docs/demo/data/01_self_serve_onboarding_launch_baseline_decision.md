# Self-serve onboarding launch Q3 baseline decision

Date: 2026-01-28

Product, engineering, and customer success reviewed the self-serve onboarding launch timing after activation instrumentation and release controls missed the January readiness gate.

## Decision
We decided to move the self-serve onboarding launch target to Q3 2026 and prioritize activation instrumentation and release-control work first.

## Assumptions
We assumed customer success could manually onboard demand for the self-serve onboarding launch through Q2 if monthly onboarding volume stayed under 40 accounts per month.

## Constraints
The self-serve onboarding launch team was limited by engineering and design capacity through Q2.
The self-serve onboarding launch required activation instrumentation before broad release.

## Commitments
Product lead will publish a revised self-serve onboarding launch roadmap by 2026-02-06.
