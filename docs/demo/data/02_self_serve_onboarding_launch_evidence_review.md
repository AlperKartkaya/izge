# Self-serve onboarding launch evidence review

Date: 2026-03-18

Growth, sales, and customer success reviewed the self-serve onboarding launch delay after support queues and enterprise trial signals worsened.

## Outcomes
Manual onboarding volume for the self-serve onboarding launch reached 68 accounts per month.
First-response time for self-serve onboarding launch requests slipped to nine days.
Enterprise trial starts fell by 14 percent after the self-serve onboarding launch delay.

## Constraints
Activation instrumentation for the self-serve onboarding launch cannot be paused, so backend capacity remains capped through the end of Q2.

## Assumptions
The team still believed manual onboarding could bridge demand until Q3 for the self-serve onboarding launch.
The evidence weakened the self-serve onboarding launch assumption that customer success could bridge demand under 40 accounts per month.
